ONE METHOD / MULTIVARIATE RESIDUALS

Features + residual vector

For a track forecast, each residual contains east and north displacement. The implementation stacks four mature vectors, plus ages and masks, and conditions the correction on this block and the issue-time features.

The main comparison is TabPFN baseline versus the complete residual-feedback method. Correction-only ablations are retained in the archive.
