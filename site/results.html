Why correction can help uncertainty without improving every position forecast

For a true vector residual r and estimated correction δ, the tangent squared-error gain is 2r·δ − ‖δ‖². Estimated corrections can point in the wrong direction or overshoot. Conformal coverage, region efficiency and position MAE are evaluated separately.
