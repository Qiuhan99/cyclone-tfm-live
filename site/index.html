01 / COMPLETE FOUNDATION-MODEL COHORT

Beyond the selected cases.

In the direct-target +24h experiment, 169 endpoints from 27 storms give feedback MAE of 227.7 km, against 284.5–474.6 km for three tested TSFMs and 217.7 km for Base TabPFN. The feedback / Base matched ellipse area ratio is 0.941×, with a whole-storm 95% interval of [0.830, 1.301]. The interval crosses 1; the independent gain from residual history is still being tested.

Information available at the issue.

Residuals and calibration labels must mature before use. The map uses archived rolling-v2 forecasts; the live model has a later feature and context configuration. Historical revised truth supports retrospective studies. The public ledger separately requires forecasts confirmed before the target.
