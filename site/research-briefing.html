01 / FORMULATION

Features, mature residuals,
then calibrated regions.

01 · Tabular representation

A pretrained model predicts displacement.

A 48-hour position history becomes tabular features. The live F2 branch adds motion, intensity and climatological features. A constant-velocity reference is removed from the target and added back after inference.

b̂(t,h) = CV(t,h) + f(X(t,h))

CV means constant velocity, not cross-validation. Frozen weights use labelled historical contexts; this is not label-free inference.

02 · Vector residual feedback

Condition on the errors already observed.

Each mature residual has east and north components. Four same-storm, same-lead vectors are transported into the current tangent frame and stacked with their ages and availability masks.

r(j,h) = u(j,h) − b̂(j,h), j + h ≤ t
R(t,h) = [r₁,…,r₄, ages, masks]
δ̂(t,h) = g([X*(t,h), R(t,h)])

X* includes the original features and Base displacement. The current target's unknown residual is never an input.

03 · Conformal calibration

Calibrate the remaining position error.

The corrected displacement is mapped back to the sphere. Mature calibration errors determine a rank threshold. Historical experiments compare circular and covariance-shaped regions.

p̂(t,h) = Expₚₜ(b̂(t,h) + δ̂(t,h))
C(t,h) = {y : s(t,h,y) ≤ q(t,h)}

The live export retains its original radial circles. Historical ellipses are a distinct protocol; native TSFM quantiles are also distinct.

A testable mechanism

Residual feedback aims to reduce predictable bias or error structure. Conformal calibration then measures the errors left over. Neither a shifted centre nor a smaller contour alone demonstrates improved coverage.

04 / EVALUATION CONTRACT

What makes a comparison interpretable.

Same forecast task

Match storm, issue, lead and valid time. Short-context TSFM experiments, old rolling-v2 illustrations and live CV-F2 are distinct configurations.

Available information only

Residual and calibration labels must have matured by the issue. Revised historical truth supports retrospective studies; it does not establish original operational availability.

Three metrics together

Endpoint position error, empirical coverage and region area answer different questions. Equal nominal targets do not imply equal realized coverage.

Uncertainty estimates

Use whole-storm paired resampling for the reported historical intervals. Overlapping origins are dependent. Rolling conformal calibration under drift is evaluated empirically, without an automatic exchangeability guarantee.

Open scientific questions & current boundaries

The main next step is a preregistered evaluation on an unexposed season and controlled distribution shifts, with aligned context, covariates and calibration budgets. Motion-change strata are proxies, not identified concept drift. F3 environmental steering and Regime-Gated correction are not active in the live pipeline.

The current site tracks CHAMPI; it is not a global operational forecast service. Research outputs predict position, without an intensity or warning head. Official wind and warnings retain their own sources.
