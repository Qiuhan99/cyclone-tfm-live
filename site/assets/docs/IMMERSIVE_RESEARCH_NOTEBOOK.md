# CYCLONE: an authored research notebook

26 October 2026 · presentation update; original experiments preserved.

The public home and Research briefing share a guided map narrative, **What the forecast leaves behind**. Authorship is visible as Qiuhan. First-person text expresses the research motivation already described in the project: whether mature forecast-error vectors carry useful information. It does not invent personal history, credentials or quotations.

Seven chapters reveal the archived observed prefix, Base forecast, observed error, full vector-feedback forecast, original region projections, selected-cohort evidence and the link to public prospective verification. The fixed map uses the existing four-storm showcase and Natural Earth coastline. Changing the case changes the actual original origin, paths and errors. Map extent is held fixed across chapters of a case; coordinates and region scales are not displaced or interpolated to imply a gain.

The plotted regions are the original frozen 90% joint-path region projections. Retrospective equal-coverage endpoint-area diagnostics remain separate and labelled. The map is an earlier rolling-v2 illustration. Current live CV-F2 has a later feature and context configuration; the map is not relabelled as current live inference.

**Present** enters a focused reading mode, with previous/next controls and left/right or Page Up/Down navigation. Escape exits. Native controls retain their own keyboard behaviour. Presentation chapter jumps are immediate, avoiding skipped stages during repeated key presses; normal reading retains browser scrolling. The reduced-motion preference disables animation and smooth navigation. No sound plays and the site does not request fullscreen or system permissions.

The complete Results, Conformal benchmark, matched CMC/UKM/NGX map, research atlas, archive and live monitor keep their existing URLs. The dossier at the end of the narrative links to complete cohorts, paired intervals and source files. Scientific JSON, model inference, calibration and verification eligibility are unchanged.
