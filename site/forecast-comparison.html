Model and region interpretation

Each experiment retains its own forecast and calibration records. Two-stage v1, direct vector feedback and external weather guidance are separate configurations. External models may use richer inputs and different release schedules. Playback shows the saved forecast, not newly computed live inference.

Shading denotes a nominal 90% prediction region for one endpoint, not a 90% probability at a single coordinate or simultaneous coverage of the entire track. Regions appear only where a matching saved calibration radius exists. Native model times may differ; missing endpoints are left unscored.
